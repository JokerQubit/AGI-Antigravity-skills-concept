## 1. Multi-Modal Engineering Philosophy & Visual Epistemology

### 1.1 The Visual Satisficing Trap
In generative AI and automated front-end engineering, visual deliverables frequently default to generic, low-contrast, or juvenile templates—flat interfaces lacking visual hierarchy, cartoonish stock imagery, and uncalibrated 3D renders.

Layer 9 enforces the **Cinema-Grade Physicality Invariant**:
> Visual and acoustic assets must emulate physical optical cameras, real world lighting physics, material absorption acoustics, and high-density industrial telemetry. Superficial prompts ("futuristic city 4k") are strictly forbidden.

### 1.2 Zero Device Frame Mandate
When generating UI/UX interfaces via `generate_image`:
- **Interface Only**: Generate strictly the UI canvas itself.
- **No Surrounding Devices**: Surrounding laptop bezels, smartphone cases, desktop monitors, desks, hands, or office environments are strictly prohibited unless the user explicitly commands a physical device mockup.

### 3.2 High-Density Telemetry Component Specs
Dashboards must maximize signal-to-noise ratio:
1. **Micro-Sparkline Graphs**: Sub-millisecond tick latency and CPU thread allocations rendered with zero visual lag.
2. **Tabular Data Grids**: Explicit column widths, monospaced tabular numbers (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`), and subtle row hover highlights.

### 3.3 Monospace Telemetry Grid Engineering Standard
When building telemetry components:
- **Zero Layout Shifts**: Metric counters must reserve fixed character widths using tabular figures (`ch` units, e.g., `width: 8ch`) to prevent cumulative layout shifts during real-time streaming updates.
- **Microsecond Timestamp Formatting**: Every log row must display timestamps formatted as `HH:mm:ss.ffffff` in muted slate (`#64748B`), with log level tags styled as colored badges (Cyan for INFO, Emerald for PASS, Amber for WARN, Crimson for HALT).

### 4.4 Spatial Acoustic Modeling & Impulse Raytracing
For interactive audio or spatial soundscapes, Layer 9 models real-time room acoustics:
- **Impulse Response Reverberation**: Sound pressure decay follows Sabine's reverberation equation:
$$RT_{60} = \frac{0.161 \cdot V}{\sum_{i} S_i \alpha_i}$$
Where $V$ is room volume in $m^3$, $S_i$ is surface area, and $\alpha_i$ is the material absorption coefficient.
- **Material Absorption Constants**:
  - Concrete / Industrial Granite: $\alpha = 0.02$ (highly reflective, crisp acoustic slapback).
  - Acoustic Absorption Panels: $\alpha = 0.85$ (damped reflection, clear vocal isolation).
  - Ballistic Laminated Glass: $\alpha = 0.04$ (high-frequency specular bounce).
- **Speed-of-Sound Delay Buffer**: Visual lightning flashes or muzzle flashes precede acoustic reports by $\Delta t = \frac{\text{Distance}}{343\,\text{m/s}}$.
