## 1. The Parable of the Devil's Apple & Epistemic Distrust

In cognitive organizations and high-stakes engineering, the greatest vulnerability is not outright failure, but **premature consensus and groupthink**:
- An employee drafts a proposal; peers nod in agreement; managers approve; the CEO gives a preliminary green light; or the user suggests a plan that sounds intuitive.
- The proposal appears attractive, polished, and ripe on the surface.
- **This is the "Devil's Apple"**: a pristine exterior concealing structural rot, unverified mathematical assumptions, latent deadlocks, or economic insolvency beneath the skin.

In the **OmniCognition Labs Ecosystem**, we enforce the **Principle of Institutional Distrust**:
- **Immediate Skepticism of Agreement**: Whenever a plan, architecture, or hypothesis completes its initial drafting and group-thinking process, the result is **never trusted immediately**.
- **Mandatory Clean-Context Red-Teaming**: No authorâ€”whether Employee, Department Head, CEO, or Userâ€”may grade their own homework or assume their draft is sound.

### Phase A: Empirical Truth & Accuracy Verification
- Are cited APIs, mathematical equations, performance figures, and data claims grounded in ground truth?
- Every assumption is treated as guilty of hallucination until verified by codebase inspection or formal proof.

### Phase B: Structural Rot Hunting (Uncovering the Realistic Flaw)
- Where will this design collapse under production load?
- What are the single points of failure, concurrency race conditions, memory leaks, and edge-case exceptions?
- What implicit human or machine dependencies are unaddressed?

### Phase C: Peak Quality Frontiering (*Via Deserti*)
- What does this plan need to transition from "good enough" to the undisputed state of the art?
- What orthogonal dimensions ($X_1 \dots X_n$) were omitted?

### Phase D: Direct Document Revision & Hardening
- The validator does **NOT** merely append a list of complaints or advisory bullet points.
- The validator **directly edits and hardens the documented content**:
  - Rewrites ambiguous interface definitions.
  - Inserts defensive validation checks and fallback procedures.
  - Rectifies flawed assumptions.
  - Embeds concrete error budgets and SLA metrics.

### Phase E: Delivery & Feedback Loop
- The fortified artifact is returned to the original author with an executive changelog highlighting every identified rot vector and the exact rationale for each modification.

## 3. Operational Invariants

1. **The Distrust Invariant**: No hypothesis or plan may advance to production implementation without an explicit, recorded sign-off from a clean-context Devil's Apple validation pass.
2. **Actionable Revision Mandate**: A critique that does not directly rewrite and improve the target artifact is incomplete and rejected.
3. **Ledger Immutability**: The original draft, the identified flaws, and the revised artifact must all be permanently logged into `.state/ledger/`.
