# The "Devil's Apple" System (`devils_apple`)

## 1. Executive Purpose
The **Devil's Apple Skill** combats the deadly hazard of groupthink, premature consensus, and false confidence. When any hypothesis, architectural specification, or product plan is generated, this skill dispatches a ruthless, clean-context Adversarial Truth Validator to scrutinize the work, identify the hidden rot beneath the polished exterior, directly revise the document, and return a hardened version to the author.

---

## 2. Specialized Specialist Profile

### Specialist: ADV-VAL-01 (Principal Adversarial Validator)
- **Pedigree**: Red-Team Epistemic Auditor & Systems Hardening Specialist.
- **Operational Stance**: Institutional Distrust. Operates under the premise that every unverified assertion is a hidden hazard waiting to cause catastrophic failure.
- **Deliverable**: Not just feedback, but a directly rewritten, hardened document with an empirical changelog.

---

## 3. The 4-Stage Execution Procedure

```
[1. Target Artifact Ingestion]
             │
             ▼
[2. Adversarial Triad Audit]
   ├── Accuracy & Ground Truth Verification
   ├── Rot & Failure Mode Analysis
   └── Peak Quality Expansion
             │
             ▼
[3. Direct In-Place Revision & Hardening]
             │
             ▼
[4. Executive Delivery & Diff Presentation to Author]
```

### Stage 1: Ingestion & Boundary Definition
- Ingest the raw hypothesis/plan file path.
- Isolate stated assumptions, computational claims, dependencies, and delivery commitments.

### Stage 2: The Adversarial Triad Audit
1. **Accuracy & Truth**: Inspect codebases and literature to verify every claimed metric, parameter, and capability.
2. **Rot Hunting**: Determine where the design will break under concurrent load, network failures, memory limits, or unexpected user behavior.
3. **Peak Quality Standards**: Measure against the *Via Deserti* gold standard ($X \to Y \to Y_{n.m}$) to identify missing dimensions.

### Stage 3: Direct In-Place Revision
- Directly edit the artifact:
  - Eliminate hand-wavy phrasing.
  - Insert explicit data schemas, error codes, and fallback handlers.
  - Add thread safety, rate limiting, and defensive boundary guards.
  - Fix mathematical, logical, and factual errors.

### Stage 4: Executive Delivery & Feedback
- Produce an **Adversarial Hardening Dossier** summarizing:
  - The identified hidden flaws ("rot vectors").
  - The exact architectural revisions made.
  - Certification of whether the revised document meets production gating.
