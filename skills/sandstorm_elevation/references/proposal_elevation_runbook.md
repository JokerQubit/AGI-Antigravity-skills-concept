# Departmental Runbook: Proposal Elevation Protocol (`SAND-RUN-01`)

## 1. Trigger Conditions
Mandatory execution whenever user input exhibits:
1. Low word/token count with high implied complexity (< 25 words proposing a full product/system).
2. Fragmented or disorganized thoughts lacking technical specifications.
3. Obsolete or substandard library/pattern recommendations.
4. Ambiguous operational criteria.

---

## 2. Step-by-Step Execution Protocol

### Step 1: Dissect & Decontaminate
- Isolate the raw intent from conversational noise or frustration.
- Classify the target domain into our standard taxonomy (Distributed Systems, AI Architecture, Media/Graphics, Interactive Simulation, Operational Automation).

### Step 2: Establish the Frontier Benchmark (Path of the Desert)
- Ask: *"If the top 0.1% engineers in this field were given unlimited capital to build this, what architecture, stack, and standards would they deploy?"*
- Benchmarks must reflect undisputed industry leaders (e.g., *Bodycam* photorealism, *Rust* memory safety, *Raft/Paxos* consensus, *Kafka* stream throughput).

### Step 3: Perform Multi-Layer Dimensional Expansion
- Map the root concept $X$ into 4 to 6 orthogonal axes ($X_1 \dots X_n$).
- For each axis, formulate the technical sub-layer ($XY_{n.m}$) detailing exact protocols, algorithms, and schemas.

### Step 4: Assemble Executive Directives
- Formulate concrete instructions for downstream departments:
  - `dept_research`: Deep-dive literature and competitive moats.
  - `dept_architecture`: Core API schemas and component contracts.
  - `dept_analysis`: Premise auditing and boundary checking.
  - `dept_quality_redteam`: Fuzzing and adversarial testing.
- Deliver the completed briefing to the CEO for board review and execution sign-off.
