# Departmental Runbook: Exploratory Intelligence Board Protocol (`GF-RUN-01`)

## 1. Trigger Conditions
Executes when a brand-new project or completely novel problem space is presented by the user, and no company documents or specific architectures have been written.

## 2. Step-by-Step Execution Protocol

### Step 1: Broad Hypothesis Mapping
- The CEO identifies the 3 core pillars of the request:
  1. *Feasibility*: Is this physically, computationally, and theoretically possible?
  2. *Novelty & Prior Art*: Has this been solved? If so, why isn't it ubiquitous?
  3. *Failure Vectors*: Where will this break under real-world conditions?

### Step 2: Parallel Deep-Dive Invocations
- Launch at least 3 parallel sub-agents with clean contexts.
- Demand exact citations, GitHub links, algorithmic complexity, and counter-arguments.

### Step 3: Synthesis & Dynamic Asset Creation
- Combine the findings into a foundational briefing.
- Automatically author the initial `rules/corporate_charter.md` and seed `.state/status.json`.
- Create domain-specific departmental skills as needed.
